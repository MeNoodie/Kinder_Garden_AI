# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Kinder Garden AI community welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome questions and good-faith feedback.
- Focus discussion on ideas and the project, not personal attacks.
- Respect privacy; never share another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, insults, trolling, sexualized language or imagery, and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this code. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

To report a concern privately, contact the repository maintainer through the contact details on the GitHub profile. Reports will be handled respectfully and confidentially where possible.
